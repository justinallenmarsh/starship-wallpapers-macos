# Artwork credits and provenance

**Starship / SpaceX:** Art credited to SpaceX and Tom Ballard. Four processed wallpapers from [Tom Ballard's Liftoff collection](https://github.com/tcballard/omarchy-theme-liftoff) at revision `dba3c7038404a7a842faad3a939dbf8006ba2c5a`:

| Wallpaper | Upstream source |
| --- | --- |

| Sunset | `backgrounds/03-liftoff-sunset.png` |
| Engines | `backgrounds/02-engine-cluster.png` |

Tom Ballard supplied or enhanced these images. Their 8K detail includes AI reconstruction. Sunset is a painted reinterpretation of a SpaceX launch photograph. [Original Liftoff credit notes](https://github.com/tcballard/omarchy-theme-liftoff/blob/dba3c7038404a7a842faad3a939dbf8006ba2c5a/CREDITS.md). The maintainer received written permission to redistribute these processed images with attribution; correspondence is held by the maintainer.

**Roadster / Tesla:** Nine images supplied and enhanced by Tom Ballard for the [Roadster 8K Omarchy theme](https://github.com/tcballard/omarchy-theme-roadster-8k) at revision `b770729ecf305d50b1b9a9c26b46dc98096290eb`. Wall Ride, Lineup, Hover, Ramp, Inverted, Rear, Diffuser, Studio, and Poster correspond to `backgrounds/01-` through `09-` in that repository. The upstream theme's [credit notes](https://github.com/tcballard/omarchy-theme-roadster-8k/blob/b770729ecf305d50b1b9a9c26b46dc98096290eb/CREDITS.md) say the original photographer, source URLs and media license are not established; the images are not covered by its MIT code license. The maintainer confirms separate permission to redistribute these processed wallpapers **on condition of credit** to Tom Ballard. That permission does not establish ownership of the original photographs or transfer any copyright.

Processing here: source SHA-256 verification, downscaling, preserving the Roadster compositions with dark margins where their proportions differ from 16:9, sRGB tagging, 10-bit HEIC compression, and native Light/Dark metadata for the Starship automatic image. [Source checksums for Starship](sources.sha256) · [Source checksums for Roadster](roadster-sources.sha256).
